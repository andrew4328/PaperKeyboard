### Paper Keyboard

Who wants to make a paper keyboard with us?

* print a piece of paper with clearly legible symbols
* OR project a legible layout onto a surface
* tap the symbols in visibility of a camera
* camera registers key presses as key ups, key holds, key downs
* finger differentiation allows for various modifiers for different hands / fingers

Toto, I’ve a feeling we’re not in QWERTY anymore!

Not really a new idea, just a backburner thing that would be necessary for other larger projects I am interested in.

Look up "laser keyboard" for the general direction.
What we are looking to elaborate on:
* print new paper = automatically new keyboard layout
* download new projection layout = automatically new keyboard layout

Not looking to sell a cheap product here, so this is just an Open Source project to leverage standard webcams or projectors.
